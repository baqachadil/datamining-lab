DBSCAN

A point is core if its ε-neighbourhood holds at least MinPts points (itself included). Clusters grow from core to core; border points join but do not expand; the rest is noise. Hover a point to see its ε-circle.

User-based collaborative filtering

Edit the ratings (1–5, empty = not rated). Pick a user and an item: the k most similar users who rated that item vote on the predicted rating, weighted by their similarity.
